pvlse

The social media of people that go out.

pvlse is a social network of real places — who is out, which rooms are alive, and how a city moves after dark. Presence unlocks the inside of a venue. A pulse is not a like. It is proof you were there.

  • Horizon map — live beacons on the places that are beating
  • The Stream — venues, streaks, and nights in your city
  • Available tonight — raise a hand, meet people who left the house
  • Live rooms — talk to the people standing in the same place as you

Not deployed yet. Private build in progress.
